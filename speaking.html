Speaking

Speaking topics

I give lectures and keynotes on AI governance and data protection law to governments, regulators, NGOs, and industry audiences. These talks focus on translating the policy and ethical questions that AI creates into concrete stakes. Topics usually include AI accuracy and fairness, algorithmic discrimination, AI in the justice system, deepfakes and other synthetic media, dark patterns and personalized manipulation, and AI transparency.
